Bhoomi Help Desk – Account & Data Deletion

Developer: Android & Coding

App Name: Bhoomi Help Desk

🔹 How to Request Account Deletion

Users can request deletion of their Bhoomi Help Desk account by following the steps below:

  • Send an email to support@androidandcoding.com
  • Use the subject line: Account Deletion Request – Bhoomi Help Desk
  • Include your registered mobile number or email ID
After verification, your account deletion request will be processed.

🔹 Data That Will Be Deleted

  • User profile information
  • Registered phone number or email
  • Shop or service details added by the user
  • Uploaded images and listings
  • User preferences and saved data

🔹 Data That May Be Retained

Some data may be retained for legal, security, or compliance purposes:

  • Basic transaction or audit logs (if applicable)

Retention period: Up to 30 days, after which the data is permanently deleted.

🔹 Processing Time

Account deletion requests are processed within 7 working days.
